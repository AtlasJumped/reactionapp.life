Reaction App

Connect your accounts

Watch where you already watch. Open a platform below in a new tab, sign in the usual way, then record a session.

Honest note: a web page cannot log in to these apps for you, so there is no sign in button here. Each button opens the official site in a new tab and marks it "opened" on this device only.

    Or send a video to a friend

    Pick a video from your device and send your friend a link. Delivering the video and getting their reaction back needs a server that we have not built yet Planned. Today you can record your own reaction to a video, and share the link with a friend so they can try it too.

    Record a session

    Choose how to record. A session runs 5 minutes. When time is up, we never cut you off.

    • When you press Start, your browser asks to use your camera, and in screen mode to share a screen, tab, or window.
    • Pick what to share. In screen mode, choose the tab or window with the video app you opened, then switch to it.
    • Nothing is uploaded. Recordings stay on this device until you save or share them.
    • You can stop at any time with the Done button.
